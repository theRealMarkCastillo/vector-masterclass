# Course data and attribution

`course/` contains original fictional teaching fixtures. Product names, policies, tenants, and support codes are invented; they do not describe real equipment or credentials. `scripts/build_course_data.py` is the authoring source. Source text is immutable within a revision; query evidence uses character offsets and SHA-256 hashes. `docs/GOLD_REVIEW.md` exposes the exact labels for review.

`downloads/scifact/` is a separately downloaded BEIR adaptation of SciFact, licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0 according to [its dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BeIR/scifact). Credit: David Wadden and the SciFact authors, and Nandan Thakur and the BEIR authors. See [SciFact](https://github.com/allenai/scifact) and [BEIR](https://github.com/beir-cellar/beir). Retain that attribution and license when sharing adapted benchmark data. The archive and extracted files are checksum-pinned in `configs/benchmark.json`; they are not bundled into Git.

The course uses the full public corpus and deterministic small query slices chosen before model evaluation. Relevance is measured against supplied judgments; unjudged results are not proof of irrelevance, and reported recall is not exhaustive semantic recall. The scientific abstracts are retrieval material, not medical guidance.

Lessons 14–19 and 23 use small original fictional fixtures defined visibly in their notebook authoring scripts: temporal recommendation events, merge-safe record pairs, scope-sensitive cache probes, intent routing, corrected memories, classification/anomaly examples, multilingual queries, parse-only Python functions, geometric images, visual pages, and a curated supply-chain graph. These are diagnostic labels/examples, not external representative benchmarks. The user's gold approval applies to the fourteen core questions; it does not imply independent human approval of every additional synthetic fixture.

Lesson 19c also uses scikit-learn's packaged `china.jpg` and `flower.jpg` sample photographs. Their upstream CC BY 2.0 attribution and original Flickr links are copied verbatim to each run's `photo-attribution.txt`. The upstream flower account naming is inconsistent; the course preserves it rather than inventing a correction. Redistributed photo outputs need that attribution. Shapes and synthetic pages are original course drawings, labeled CC0 in the lesson. No external page corpus or research embeddings are silently bundled.

Model repository IDs, immutable revisions, weight filenames, dimensions, tokenizer contracts, and licenses are recorded in `configs/models.json`. Full-course models use Apache-2.0 or MIT licenses according to their pinned source records; CLIP's license is linked to its upstream repository. Download manifests record checksums and byte counts. Package licenses remain those of their respective distributions; MathJax's pinned render asset is Apache-2.0. A project-wide redistribution license for the course itself has not been selected.
